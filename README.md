### About Me

I work on **computer vision**, especially **all-in-one image restoration**, blind restoration, degradation-aware modeling, and research systems that make academic work easier to reproduce, review, and extend.

I am currently affiliated with **Zhongnan University of Economics and Law**.

My recent interests include:

- Unified restoration models for multiple degradations
- Prompt-based, MoE-based, diffusion-based, and Mamba-style restoration methods
- Evaluation protocols for real-world and mixed-degradation restoration
- Research agents for literature scouting, peer review, rebuttal planning, and academic writing

---

### Research Focus

| Area | What I Care About |
| --- | --- |
| All-in-One Image Restoration | Building general restoration models that handle rain, haze, blur, noise, low light, and mixed degradations |
| Blind Image Restoration | Designing models that infer degradation types and strengths without clean metadata |
| Low-Level Vision | Improving perceptual quality, fidelity, robustness, and practical usability |
| Research Tooling | Automating paper discovery, review analysis, writing polish, and reproducible experiment planning |
